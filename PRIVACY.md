# Privacy Policy

UniCafe Daily is designed as a frontend-only Android application.

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, contacts, or precise location.

Favourite restaurants, app preferences, and optional local features are stored locally on the device.

The app connects to UniCafe's public menu service to retrieve menu and restaurant information.

This policy should be updated if the application later adds analytics, crash reporting, advertising, authentication, or other third-party services.
